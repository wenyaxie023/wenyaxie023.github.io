Wenya Xie

Ph.D. candidate @ UMN CSE · Data and evaluation for LLM agents

I’m Wenya Xie, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Minnesota, advised by Prof. Zirui Liu. Previously, I was a research assistant at CUHK (Shenzhen) with Prof. Benyou Wang and Feng Jiang.

I work on data and evaluation for LLM agents. Agents will only be everywhere once they are cheap to build and run, so the question I care about is how much data and how large a model it takes to do a task reliably, and how to measure that. So far I’ve worked on synthetic data for long-horizon evaluation, wasted compute in reasoning models, and reproducible evaluation; I’m now building mid-training data.

I’m looking for a research internship starting as early as January 2027, and I’m flexible on length (6 to 12 months). Email me.

current work

I’m training a model to predict individual human decisions. So far I have collected real decision data organized by a task taxonomy, and I’m synthesizing reasoning traces.
